Company Overview

Radtrix Automation LLP is a technology-driven engineering company specializing in Industrial Automation, Instrumentation, Process Control, Telemetry, Industrial IoT (IIoT), Smart Monitoring Systems, and Digital Transformation Solutions.

Established as a sister concern of Chem-Tech Associates and Tanay Sales Corporation, Radtrix Automation LLP combines decades of industry expertise with modern automation technologies to deliver innovative, reliable, and cost-effective solutions to industries, utilities, infrastructure projects, and municipal organizations across India.

Backed by the legacy of Chem-Tech Associates (32 Years) and Tanay Sales Corporation (29 Years), Radtrix Automation LLP leverages over 60 years of collective industry experience to provide advanced monitoring, automation, and control solutions that help organizations improve efficiency, reliability, productivity, and sustainability.

With a strong focus on intelligent monitoring, remote data acquisition, process optimization, and digital transformation, we help customers make informed decisions through real-time data, smart analytics, and automation technologies.

Our commitment is to deliver high-quality engineering solutions supported by technical excellence, innovation, and long-term customer relationships.

Group Strength

Radtrix Automation LLP is built upon the strong foundation of two established organizations that have successfully served Indian industries for decades.

Chem-Tech Associates – 32 Years of Excellence

With a proud legacy of over 32 years, Chem-Tech Associates has earned a reputation as a trusted partner for industrial instrumentation, flow measurement, analytical instrumentation, process control, and engineering solutions. Through its commitment to quality products, technical expertise, and customer satisfaction, the company has successfully served industries, utilities, consultants, contractors, and infrastructure projects across India.

Tanay Sales Corporation – 29 Years of Industry Presence

With 29 years of market presence, Tanay Sales Corporation has been a reliable supplier and solution provider for industrial, municipal, and infrastructure sectors. The company has built strong relationships with customers and technology partners through its focus on quality products, dependable service, and long-term customer support.

Radtrix Automation LLP – The Next Generation
