ELECTROMAGNETIC FLOW METER

Technical Specification:

  • Flow Meter Size: 15 mm to 500 mm
  • Measuring Range: 0.2 to 10 m/sec. Bidirectional
  • Media: Conductive Liquid/Water, Slurry
  • Required Electrical Conductivity: >10 uS/cm
  • Operating Temperature: 0°C 60°C

Flow Sensor (Primary Head):

  • Flow meter Measuring Principle: Faraday's law of Electromagnetic induction
  • Flow Meter Type: Full Bore Electromagnetic flow meter
  • Excitation Type: Pulsed DC
  • Power Supply: 230 V AC, 50 Hz or 24 V DC
  • Connections: Flange End
  • Flange Pressure Rating: ASA 150
  • Flange Material: CS/MS/SS
  • Flow Sensor Pressure Rating: 10 kg/cm2 or as per Customer requirement
  • Temperature: 70°C (for Rubber), 120°C (for PTFE)
  • Material: Rubber/PTFE
  • Electrode Material: SS 316/Hastalloy C
  • MOC of Flow Tube/Meter Tube: SS 304
  • Sensor/Coil Housing (Shell): MS/SS 304/SS 316
  • Protection Class for Sensor: IP 67, IP 68 (only with Remote Version)

Flow Sensor (Primary Head):

  • Type: Microprocessor based Integral/Remote Version
  • Type of Display: 2 Line Backlit LCD display.
  • Display Parameter: Instantaneous Flow, Total Flow with sum, forward and reverse total counters
  • Accuracy: +/-0.5% of full scale reading
  • From flow Sensor
  • Output: 4-20 mA, Pulse and MODBUS 485
  • Enclosure MOC: Die Cast Aluminium
  • Protection Class: IP 65, IP 67
  • Through push button
  • Cable Entry: M20X15, Polyamide Cable Glands
  • Protocol: MODBUS RS 485 and HART (Optional)
