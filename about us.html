ABOUT US

Company Overview

Radtrix Automation LLP is a technology-driven engineering company specializing in Industrial Automation, Instrumentation, Process Control, Telemetry, Industrial IoT (IIoT), Smart Monitoring Systems, and Digital Transformation Solutions.
Established as a sister concern of Chem-Tech Associates and Tanay Sales Corporation, Radtrix Automation LLP combines decades of industry expertise with modern automation technologies to deliver innovative, reliable, and cost-effective solutions to industries, utilities, infrastructure projects, and municipal organizations across India.
Backed by the legacy of Chem-Tech Associates (32 Years) and Tanay Sales Corporation (29 Years), Radtrix Automation LLP leverages over 60 years of collective industry experience to provide advanced monitoring, automation, and control solutions that help organizations improve efficiency, reliability, productivity, and sustainability. With a strong focus on intelligent monitoring, remote data acquisition, process optimization, and digital transformation, we help customers make informed decisions through real-time data, smart analytics, and automation technologies.

Commitment is to deliver high-quality engineering solutions supported by technical excellence, innovation, and long-term customer relationships.

Vision & Mission

To become a trusted leader in industrial automation, instrumentation, and smart monitoring solutions by delivering innovative technologies that enhance productivity, sustainability, and operational excellence.

To provide reliable and innovative automation solutions tailored to customer requirements.

To build long-term partnerships through trust, quality, and customer satisfaction.
To continuously adopt emerging technologies that create measurable value for our customers.
